## 1. What was done

### 1.1 `docs/algorithms/fea.md` §2.5 rewritten (the Round-3 brief item #1)

The old section claimed "one HEX8 through the thickness is ~an order of magnitude too
stiff — require ≥2, or use shells". That was wrong on two counts after R2-O1: the plain
element is ~64 % of reference (a third too stiff, not 10×), and the *default* formulation
is now Wilson/Taylor incompatible modes, which makes the one-layer rule obsolete. The
section (retitled "HEX8 — trilinear solid, incompatible modes by default") now documents:

- the shear-locking baseline: `"full"` 2×2×2 recovers **0.6428** of the Timoshenko tip
  deflection on the 10×1×1 slender cantilever (one element through the thickness);
- the default incompatible-modes formulation (Q6/QM6): nine internal bubble DOFs,
  static condensation $K = K_{uu} - K_{ua} K_{aa}^{-1} K_{ua}^\top$,
  Taylor–Beresford–Wilson centre-Jacobian correction ⇒ distorted-mesh patch test exact;
- the corrected headline: **one through-thickness element reaches ~98.6 %**
  (0.9855 at 10×1×1, 0.9601 already at 4×1×1);
- no hourglass modes (PSD Schur complement argument + free–free 6-rigid-mode check);
  internal DOFs carry no mass; ~+13 % assembly cost;
- the three selectable formulations (`incompatible`/`bbar`/`full`) with the honest
  limits from R2-O1 §3: `bbar` over-softens thin bending (1.20 at 10×1×1, diverging),
  incompatible degrades near incompressibility (0.84 at ν=0.499 thin) and under
  parallelogram distortion (0.98 → 0.36 as skew → 0.4);
- pointers to `femtools.fea.verification` and `tests/test_hex8_verification.py`.

Also touched in the same file: §7 complexity table row notes the per-element 9×9
condensation; §8 verification hooks gained the distorted patch test and the
anti-locking/rigid-body hooks. All quoted numbers re-measured on this tree (see §2.3
below), not copied blind from the R2 report.
