## 4. Algorithm notes added (public literature, matching existing style)

- `docs/algorithms/updating.md` §6 (complexity renumbered to §7): first-order parameter
  covariance — sandwich formula, Gauss–Markov collapse to $(S^\top C_z^{-1} S)^{-1}$,
  distinction vs the Bayesian posterior already in `UpdateResult.covariance`,
  variance-of-unit-weight scaling, and the closed-form anchor
  $\sigma_{\hat p} = 2\sigma_f/\sqrt{n_z}$ on the E-recovery golden; seeded Monte Carlo
  (`seed` required) as linearization cross-check. Refs: Friswell & Mottershead 1995;
  Friswell, MSSP 3(2) 1989.
- `docs/algorithms/optimization.md` §4 (complexity renumbered to §5): node-based shape
  optimization — coordinate perturbations as design variables, semi-analytic element-patch
  $\partial K/\partial s$ + Fox–Kapoor / self-adjoint compliance gradients (incl. the
  Barthelemy–Haftka rigid-rotation accuracy trap), min-scaled-Jacobian barrier with KS
  aggregation, Laplacian smoothing of non-design nodes, jagged-boundary regularization.
  Refs: Haftka & Grandhi CMAME 57 (1986); Haftka & Gürdal 1992.
- `docs/algorithms/mpe_rbpe.md` §7 (complexity renumbered to §8): data-driven SSI — past/
  future output Hankel, orthogonal projection $\mathcal P_i = Y_f/Y_p = \mathcal O_i \hat
  X_i$, LQ implementation (never form $YY^\top$; SVD on the $L_{21}$ block), UPC/PC/CVA
  weightings, then the same SVD + shift-invariance path and result type as `ssi_cov`;
  cov-vs-data trade-offs and reference channels. Refs: Van Overschee & De Moor 1996;
  Peeters & De Roeck MSSP 13(6) 1999.
- `docs/algorithms/io.md` (new): Abaqus INP and LS-DYNA K text subsets — card tables
  mapped to `FEModel` (element map C3D8→HEX8 … T3D2→BAR2; K PART indirection,
  *NODE I8+3E16 fixed columns, TC/RC lookup-table codes, degenerate tet/tria retyping,
  fixed-vs-free format rule, out-of-scope long/I10 formats), aggregated-warning and
  stop-at-*STEP conventions, round-trip + cross-format K/M identity acceptance. Public
  card layouts only; OP2/RST/ODB explicitly out of scope.
