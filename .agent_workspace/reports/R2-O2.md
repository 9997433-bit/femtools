## Headline number — 20 retained modes

**rel L2 = 3.01e-2 (3.0 %), inside the 5 % contract target.**

40-DOF fixed-fixed chain (`m = 1`, `k = 1e4`), full basis 15.9155–35.5662 Hz, truncated to
its lowest **20 modes**, modal damping `zeta = 0.01`, drive at DOF 39, all 40 responses,
240 lines.

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `fmax` = last **retained** mode (f20) | **26.8358 Hz** |
| comparison band 0.2–0.8 fmax | **5.3672–21.4687 Hz** |
| `rel_l2` = ‖H_modal − H_direct‖_F / ‖H_direct‖_F | **3.0112e-02** |
| same case, band anchored on the *parent* fmax (35.5662 Hz → 7.1132–28.4529 Hz) | 1.3345e-01 (fails) |
| complete 40-mode basis, same band | **1.7644e-14** |

The parent-fmax row is the point of the whole change: the band top of 28.45 Hz sits past
four poles (f21…f24) that the 20-mode sum does not contain, so a comparison defined that
way measures missing resonances instead of residual flexibility. Anchoring on the last
retained mode is what makes the 5 % acceptance criterion well posed. This confirms and
quantifies leftover defect #9 in `ROUND1_BRIEF.md`.

Truncation sweep on the same model, each row using its own retained band:

| n_modes | fmax (Hz) | band (Hz) | rel L2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 16.6763 | 3.3353–13.3410 | 6.61e-1 |
| 10 | 19.3567 | 3.8713–15.4854 | 2.59e-1 |
| **20** | **26.8358** | **5.3672–21.4687** | **3.01e-2** |
| 30 | 32.9988 | 6.5998–26.3991 | 9.98e-3 |
| 40 (complete) | 35.5662 | 7.1132–28.4529 | 1.76e-14 |

The retained band is necessary but not sufficient: below ~20 modes the truncated sum is
genuinely too poor for 5 % on this model, so the contract line is specifically a 20-mode
statement. Convergence is monotone and the complete basis collapses to round-off, which is
the signature of pure modal truncation error rather than a formulation mismatch.

Damping sensitivity at 20 modes (all pass): `zeta = 0.005` → 2.13e-2, `zeta = 0.01` →
3.01e-2, `zeta = 0.02` → 4.27e-2, Rayleigh `alpha = 0.5, beta = 1e-5` → 1.60e-2, structural
`eta = 0.02` → 3.01e-2. Heavier damping is *worse*, not better: it broadens the poles above
the band so their tails leak further into it. Sparse `K`/`M` give 3.0112e-2, identical to
dense.

`max_rel_pointwise` is reported but is not a useful acceptance metric here (1.25e13 at 20
modes, and 3.4e-2 even with the complete basis): truncation shifts the antiresonances by a
hair, and any pointwise ratio blows up where `|H_direct|` passes through its notch. Only the
energy-weighted L2 norm is meaningful.

## What changed in `src/femtools/dynamics`

`frf.py` — three new documented helpers, exported from `femtools.dynamics`:

- `retained_fmax_hz(modal)` — highest retained natural frequency in Hz. Duck-typed input
  (`ModalModel`, `ModalResult`, mapping, `(freq_hz, modes)` tuple) via `as_modal`. Rejects a
  purely rigid-body basis, which cannot define a band.
- `retained_band(modal, low=0.2, high=0.8)` — `(low * fmax, high * fmax)` in Hz, defaulting
  to the `docs/CONTRACT_API.md` band. Validates `0 <= low < high`.
- `retained_band_lines(modal, n_lines=240, low=0.2, high=0.8)` — linearly spaced lines
  across that band.

`verify_modal_vs_direct` gained keyword-only `n_modes`, `fmax_hz`, `n_lines` and
`band_fractions`, `freq_hz` now defaults to `None`, and `band` additionally accepts the
string `"retained"`. The whole acceptance case is now one call:

```python
res = verify_modal_vs_direct(K, M, modal_full, damping=0.01, inputs=39, n_modes=20)
assert res["rel_l2"] < 0.05          # 3.01e-02
res["fmax_hz"], res["band_hz"]        # 26.8358, (5.3672, 21.4687)
```

Semantics worth knowing:

- `n_modes`/`fmax_hz` truncate the basis **for the modal side only**. The direct side keeps
  the complete `K`/`M`, and the complete basis is also what `ModalDamping` projects into the
  physical `C = M Phi diag(2 zeta_r omega_r) Phi^T M`. Both sides therefore carry the same
  damping and differ by modal truncation alone. Passing an already-truncated basis instead
  builds `C` from that subspace only; on this model it barely matters (3.0118e-2 vs
  3.0112e-2), but the intent is clearer with `n_modes`.
- When `freq_hz is None` the lines come from `retained_band_lines` of the truncated basis,
  and the band defaults to `"retained"` whenever the basis was truncated here or the axis
  was generated. An explicit `freq_hz` with `band=None` on a complete basis keeps the old
  behaviour of comparing every line.
- New return keys: `fmax_hz`, `band_hz`, `n_modes_full`. `n_modes` now reports the
  *retained* count. `rel_l2`, `abs_l2`, `max_rel_pointwise`, `n_freq`, `modal`, `direct` are
  unchanged, and the old positional call `verify_modal_vs_direct(K, M, modal, freq_hz,
  damping, inputs, outputs, band)` still works.

The module and package docstrings now state the `fmax` rule directly, so the trap is
documented where a reader of `frf.py` meets it rather than only in a Round 1 report.
