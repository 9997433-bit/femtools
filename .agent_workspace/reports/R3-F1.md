## Residual SOTA gaps (the only open items after this pass)

1. **HEX8 mesh-distortion sensitivity.** Incompatible modes degrade with skew (bending
   ratio 0.986 → ~0.36 at skew 0.4). Simo–Rifai assumed-strain stabilization is the
   distortion-robust upgrade; not implemented.
2. **`bbar` HEX8 variant too soft in thin bending** (~2.13× reference); usable only for
   near-incompressible thick parts. No guard prevents misuse.
3. **Truncated-FRF 5% band is contractual only at ≥ ~20 retained modes, light damping**;
   fewer modes or ζ ≈ 0.02 needs residual-term compensation.
4. **Reduction/expansion (Guyan/IRS/SEREP) absent** — cross-orthogonality needs caller-side
   M at common DOFs; pretest cannot build test-DOF system matrices.
5. **UNV materials are femtools-private** (dataset 30000): third-party readers skip them;
   BDF still collapses TET10/HEX20 to TET4/HEX8.
6. **Missing estimator/method families** (all R3+ in the map): free-interface CMS, complex
   modes, random/PSD response, exciter placement, FMAC, correlation reports, FRF-based and
   UQ updating, subset selection, surrogates, shape/multi-objective optimization,
   MIMO H1/H2 FRF estimation, SSI, RBPE inertia-restraint, vendor result drivers
   (OP2/rst/odb/k).
7. **CLI `pretest` scores raw active DOFs** — `pretest.candidates.translational_dofs`
   exists but `cli.py` (R3-F4-owned) does not use it yet.
8. **Static typing informational debt**: mypy still reports findings in internal modules;
   the CI step stays non-blocking by design.
9. **ACCEPTANCE.md carries ~12 untested cases** (R3-F3/G1 scope this round).
10. **DAQ hardware acquisition remains N/A by design** (synthetic-data substitute is the
    supported path).
