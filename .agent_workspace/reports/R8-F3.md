## 3. Algorithm notes (public literature, matching existing style)

- `docs/algorithms/fea.md` **§12** RBE3 interpolation — one dependent node's listed
  components as the weighted average `u_d,c = Σ w̄_i u_i,c` (normalized weights, equal
  by default), imposed by the same Cook ch. 13 / Zienkiewicz–Taylor master–slave
  elimination as §11 but explicitly *not* the RBE2 rigid-weld kinematics and with no
  penalty springs; composition of `model.rbe2` + `model.rbe3` into **one**
  `ConstraintTransform` (merged rows before chain resolution, `mpc=False` disables all,
  empty `rbe3` bit-identical to the RBE2-only path); why 6 free–free RBM hold with no
  geometric condition, plus the weighted-centroid caveat for inertia placement; equal
  force shares under `G^T f`; scope note vs the full Nastran least-squares lever-arm
  RBE3 (out of subset, never faked with RBE2 kinematics).
- `docs/algorithms/fea.md` **§13** nodal averaging — the 1/n_adj incidence average of
  §10 centroid tensors; average in the basic frame (element frames are not comparable),
  recompute von Mises/principals from the averaged tensor (never average the
  invariant), skipped elements don't vote; the constant-patch machine-precision gate;
  explicit scope guard: **not** ZZ-SPR (IJNME 33, 1992) and not Hinton–Campbell global
  smoothing.
- `docs/algorithms/io.md` **§5** BDF RBE3 card — public MSC/NX layout
  `EID (blank) REFGRID REFC WT1 C1 G1,1 …` with the type-driven tail disambiguation
  (grids integer, weights real, component strings digit-packed); flattened mapping onto
  the merged `FEModel.add_rbe3` (validation stays in the container); aggregated-warning
  subset boundaries (heterogeneous per-group components, `UM` continuation, `ALPHA`,
  non-positive weights); `write_bdf` emission + exact round-trip invariant.
- `docs/algorithms/io.md` **§6** solver text drivers — `AnsysCdbDriver` /
  `AbaqusInpDriver` as `SolverDriver` adapters (contract table: `write_cdb`/`write_inp`,
