## The one thing that changed: `enforced=`

A stress residual only carries information about `E` if the load case makes
stress depend on it. Round 8 already knew this and drove its patches with
`solver_kwargs={"enforced": ...}`; Round 9 promotes that to a named argument,
because burying the *precondition of the whole method* inside an opaque
pass-through dict is how a user ends up updating against a residual with a zero
Jacobian and believing the answer.

```python
f = static_stress_response(model, parameters, component="xx",
                           enforced={(tip, 0): 1.0e-4})
```

* bit-identical to the `solver_kwargs` spelling (asserted with `np.array_equal`);
* the mapping is copied at build time, so mutating the caller's dict afterwards
  cannot silently change the load case a built response solves;
* `enforced=` together with `solver_kwargs={"enforced": ...}` raises, and so does
  `enforced=` together with a custom `solver=` — the latter replaces
  `solve_static`, so the prescribed displacements would otherwise be dropped in
  silence;
* purely additive: every Round-8 call site still works unchanged, and
  `tests/test_round8_o4.py` was not touched.

The docstring now states the physics in the `enforced` entry rather than as an
aside.

## Why displacement-driven, asserted rather than noted

| Drive | Measured quantity | Depends on `E`? | Recovers the 10 % error |
|---|---|---|---|
| dead load `F` | stress `σ = F/A` | **no** (drift 6.7e-16 over `p ∈ [0.5, 3.7]`) | no — 9.99e-2, i.e. still 10 % out |
| dead load `F` | strain `ε = F/(A E)` | yes | 5.55e-16 |
| enforced `δ` | stress `σ = E δ/L` | yes, exactly linear | 1.11e-16 |
| enforced `δ` | strain `ε = δ/L` | **no** (drift 4.4e-16) | — |

Both blind rows are pinned, and the dead-load stress case is asserted as a
*negative control*: the update stops at the first iteration with the parameter
still ~10 % out and a finite-difference Jacobian of 3.2e-4 against a 3.3e7
response (9.5e-12 relative — pure round-off). If a future change ever made that
case appear to converge, the response would have stopped being a stress.

Linearity in the modulus is pinned directly on the HEX8 patch:
`σ(p) = p·σ(1)` **bit for bit** for dyadic `p` (0.5, 2, 4) and to 1e-15 for
0.3 and 3.7. That exactness is why the recoveries below are round-off limited
rather than tolerance limited.

### The patches themselves

* **HEX8** — 3×3×3 grid on unequal stations, 8 bricks, the 26 outer nodes driven
  with the exact linear field `u = G·x` and the enclosed node displaced
  0.09/−0.07/0.08 off centre and left free. The free node lands on the linear
  field to 2.7e-20 absolute; von Mises spread across the 8 elements is 3.56e-16
  relative; the recovered tensor matches `D(E)·ε` to 4.7e-16. All six Voigt
  components are checked against `solid_D(E)·ε`, not just `xx`.
* **BAR2** — the 1-D analogue: a 4-rod chain on unequal stations stretched by a
  prescribed tip displacement. Axial force is constant through the chain, so σ
  is constant (spread 3.99e-16) and equals `E δ/L = 1.4e7` to 1e-12.

## Notes for the parent

* `static_stress_response` is ready for top-level `_EXPORTS` — it imports on this
  tree and its signature is now gated. `enforced=` is part of the frozen
  signature as of this branch, so PRODUCT_MAP / CONTRACT_API rows should quote
  the twelve-argument form above.
* The dead-load negative control is the line worth carrying into the algorithm
  notes: *a stress residual needs a displacement-driven load case, a strain
  residual needs a force-driven one.* Under dead load a statically determinate
  structure is `σ = F/A` regardless of `E`; under enforced displacement it is
  `ε = δ/L` regardless of `E`. Choosing the wrong pair does not error, it just
  learns nothing.
